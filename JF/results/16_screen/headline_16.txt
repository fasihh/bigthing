Reading
- Learned parity check is the best detector+action: beats JF15's loo everywhere it helps, at no clean
  cost, and now also helps MOSEI align (loo did nothing there) and the variational-target model.
- On plain V-JEPA it detects better (COCO 0.70->0.82 parity, 0.84 spe; MOSEI 0.39->0.60) but the
  fused prediction does not change: the corruptions barely hurt these models' PoE to begin with
  (dead/noise NLL ~ clean+0.06), so there is little to recover.
- Widening and imputation are not worth it (over-widening; imputation unstable).
- Novelty: parity-space residuals come from analytical-redundancy fault diagnosis; cross-modal
  detect-reject-correct exists (Lee et al. 2020, reconstruction-based). Using a learned parity check on
  frozen expert latents to temper precision inside a PoE, training-free, is an incremental combination.
