Findings: (1) the toy winner (stacked mean+sigma head) overfits on real data (only ~900 clips to learn from) and is
worse than PoE; (2) the held-out corruptions barely hurt PoE on MOSEI (held NLL 1.43 vs clean 1.39), so there is little
headroom; even dropping the true culprit does not beat PoE; (3) simple conformal calibration that includes
self-generated corruptions is the most reliable gain (coverage gap 0.046 -> 0.013), but it is a known technique;
(4) dead sensor and two noisy inputs are where PoE loses (vjepa_vt dead: PoE 1.495, pattern-BMA 1.430, oracle 1.417).
